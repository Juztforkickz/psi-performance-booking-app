# PSI App privacy collection notices

Status: implementation draft, 2 September 2026. These short notices supplement
the full App Privacy Policy. They do not replace it.

## Sign-in and account access

> PSI uses your email address to verify your identity, protect your private
> customer account and send one-time sign-in codes. Codes expire and work once.
> PSI does not create or store an App password. See the PSI App Privacy Policy.

Placement: immediately before the customer requests a sign-in code.

## Profile and vehicle details

> PSI uses these details to identify you and your vehicle, provide private
> customer records and prefill workshop requests. Required details must be
> completed before account bookings can be submitted. See the PSI App Privacy
> Policy for access, correction, retention and deletion information.

Placement: above the first profile/vehicle save action.

## Booking request

> PSI collects the contact, vehicle, technical and scheduling information in
> this request so the workshop can assess the work, contact you and administer
> the proposed booking. A request is not a confirmed appointment and does not
> take payment. Relevant booking and workshop records may be retained for
> business, taxation, safety and dispute purposes. See the PSI App Privacy
> Policy.

Placement: beside the final booking consent and policy acknowledgement.

## Camera and photo library

> If you continue, your device will ask for camera or photo-library access. PSI
> receives only the photograph or file you actively capture or select for
> upload. Check that it does not contain passwords, sign-in codes, payment-card
> details or unnecessary information about another person.

Placement: immediately before opening the camera/photo choice for profile,
vehicle, dyno, invoice or repair content.

## Device notifications

> If enabled, PSI registers this device with its notification providers so it
> can deliver booking, workshop and PSI Events alerts, sounds and badge counts.
> A push token and device platform are linked to your PSI account. Notification
> content may appear on the lock screen. You can disable device alerts or
> individual categories at any time.

Placement: immediately above the Enable device alerts control.

## PSI Events reminders

> Event alerts are optional. If enabled, PSI uses your notification preference
> and registered device to tell you when an event is published, changed or
> cancelled. Disabling event alerts does not affect booking communications.

Placement: beside the PSI Events notification toggle.

## Profile photograph

> Your profile photograph is optional, private to your account and authorised
> PSI staff, and stored with PSI's protected App files. Replacing or removing it
> removes the active copy, subject to restricted backup rotation.

Placement: beside Add photo / Change photo.

## Vehicle photographs and documents

> This upload is optional and will be linked to the selected vehicle. Customer
> files are private to the account and authorised PSI staff. PSI-authored
> invoices, dyno graphs and workshop documents may remain read-only to protect
> record integrity.

Placement: immediately before the upload action in My Garage and Vehicle
Reports.

## Account deletion

> Requesting deletion starts PSI's verified review process. PSI normally
> completes a verified request within 30 days. App access, customer profile,
> notification registrations and customer-uploaded files will be removed.
> Minimum records required for taxation, workshop, safety, warranty, legal or
> dispute purposes may be restricted and retained. You can cancel while the
> request remains pending.

Placement: before the final account-deletion confirmation.

## Support email

> This opens your email app. Information you include will be sent through your
> email provider to PSI and handled as a support or privacy enquiry. Do not send
> a password, sign-in code or authenticator secret.

Placement: before opening a pre-addressed support or deletion email when a
clear intermediate notice is practical.

## Implementation rules

- Every notice must link to the dedicated PSI App Privacy Policy, not only the
  general Shopify store policy.
- Do not preselect optional permissions or marketing consent.
- Keep booking/service communications separate from direct marketing consent.
- Update the relevant notice before collecting a new category of information or
  introducing payments, analytics, advertising, precise location, contacts,
  microphone access or another provider.
- Test the notices on the smallest supported iPhone and Android layout.
