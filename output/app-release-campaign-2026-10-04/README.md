# PSI app release campaign

Prepared 4 October 2026 after public Google Play production release was confirmed active.

## Ready to use

* `PSI-Android-Launch-Feed-4x5.png` is the Android launch image for Instagram and Facebook feeds.
* `PSI-Android-Launch-Reel-Story-9x16.png` is the static vertical Story and Reel cover.
* `PSI-Android-Launch-Reel-9x16.mp4` is the silent eight second vertical Reel. Add licensed music inside Instagram or Facebook if desired.
* `PSI-Google-Play-Now-Available-Reel-9x16.mp4` is the clean singular Google Play announcement animation.
* `PSI-Google-Play-Now-Available-Cover-9x16.png` is its matching Reel cover and static Story artwork.
* `PSI-App-Download-Apple-Android-QR-Feed-4x5.png` is the combined Apple and Android social post.
* `PSI-App-Download-Apple-Android-QR-Website-1600x900.png` is the combined website graphic.
* `PSI-Facebook-Banner-Apple-Android-2033x774.jpg` preserves the approved PSI Facebook banner layout and uses one compact QR code for both stores.
* `Captions.txt` contains the prepared Android and combined store captions.

## QR files

* `assets/psi-app-store-apple-qr-1600.png` opens the public App Store listing.
* `assets/psi-google-play-qr-1600.png` opens the public Google Play listing.
* `assets/psi-app-download-both-qr-1600.png` opens the PSI download page, which now presents both stores.

The official App Store and Google Play badges are preserved as supplied by Apple and Google. Store names, URLs and QR codes were added as exact graphic elements after the workshop background was created.
