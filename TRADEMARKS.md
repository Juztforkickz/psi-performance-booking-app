# PSI Performance trade mark notice

PSI Performance™ and the associated PSI logo are used in this application as
unregistered trade marks for the PSI Performance automotive workshop in
Pakenham, Victoria, Australia.

The ™ symbol records a trade mark claim. It does not state or imply that the
name or logo is registered, registrable, or enforceable. The ® symbol must not
be used unless IP Australia has registered the exact mark for the relevant
goods or services.

No permission is granted to use the PSI Performance name, logo, visual
identity, or confusingly similar branding outside authorised PSI Performance
business activity. Third-party trade marks remain the property of their
respective owners.

## Formal Australian registration handoff

Before an application can be filed safely, PSI must confirm:

- the full legal owner of the mark; a business or trading name alone may not be
  the correct applicant;
- whether protection is sought for the words, the logo, or both;
- the exact goods and services to cover and their classes;
- an Australian or New Zealand address for service; and
- the application route and payment authority.

Likely classes for professional review include vehicle repair and tuning
services, parts retail, downloadable mobile software, and hosted software.
PSI-branded vehicle parts may require separate goods coverage. The final class
selection must match the business's actual and intended use.

Search the words, close variations, and logo through Australian Trade Mark
Search before filing. IP Australia recommends its TM Headstart service for
first-time applicants because an examiner provides feedback before the
application is formalised.
